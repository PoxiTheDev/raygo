Guide for Raygo

Commands:
    raygo doctor - checks your dependencies
    raygo new example_name - creates a new project
    raygo build - builds the project
    raygo run - builds & runs the project

Project structure:
    example_name/
        build/
        src/
            main.c
        .gitignore
        compile_flags.txt

That's basically it. You mastered raygo!
(NOTE: I may add things, if you pull a new version of raygo you should always look at the guide.txt, since whenever im gonna update this its probably gonna be a bigger update.)
